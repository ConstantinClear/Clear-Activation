41 Years. One Story. 30 Minutes. Cleared.

It was 1984 in the Romanian mountains. I was one year old. My mother was carrying me down a dock when the dock gave in. We both fell into the freezing water.

That was the moment a story was born. A story of fear. A story of "cold water is dangerous." A story that became "that's just who I am." And a part of me that took on the role of protector.

I carried that for 41 years. And it served a purpose.

It kept me safe. Until its work was done.

Then came February 2024. I was at Sensea Nordic Spa in Nova Scotia, sitting in a hot tub at minus 25 degrees Celsius, staring at a cold plunge. For six months I had been pushing through 30 miserable seconds at a time, fighting fire with fire. That day, something was different.

In 30 minutes, the story released.

I didn't fix it. I cleared the noise. I walked into the water and giggled like the one-year-old boy should have.

When I told my mother weeks later what I had witnessed in that release, she confirmed every detail. The age. The scenery. The dock. The water. Forty-one years of memory, gifted to me before I was old enough to hold it.

So I asked: if this is possible, what else is possible?

That question began the journey of CLEAR. For myself. For hundreds of others. People watching their businesses move differently, their relationships soften, doors opening that were not opening before. Not because they pushed harder. Because they stopped carrying what was never theirs.
